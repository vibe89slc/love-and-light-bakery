Sugar House · Salt Lake City

Small batches, big welcome

We’re a neighborhood bakery in Sugar House built around really good donuts — fluffy yeast donuts, cake donuts, and old fashioneds as big as your hopes, with glazes that change by the week. Ingredients stay thoughtful: no artificial dyes (you might spot beet in a pink “Homer”), real espresso drinks, cinnamon rolls, muffins, and a few nostalgic treats. Everything is plant-based and dairy-free, so vegan guests can order anything on the board — we just don’t hang the whole brand on a label. We also keep gluten-friendly picks in the rotation when we can; since the kitchen works with flour, ask what’s available that day if you’re avoiding gluten. Fritter Friday is our weekly fruit-fritter flex—flavors usually hit Instagram first, and the good stuff moves fast.

Our story

About Love & Light

We’re a tiny bakery with a loud love for dough, glaze, and the people who line up for both. Everything is made in small batches so freshness wins over flash.

Why we’re here

Love & Light started as a simple idea: serve donuts and coffee that feel handmade—craggy edges, real vanilla, espresso with crema you can see. We keep the menu plant-based because it’s how we like to cook, and we’d like to provide the healthier option to everyone’s favorite dessert. Who doesn’t love a good donut? Especially one where you won’t feel guilty for eating right after. Each glaze and each donut is never made with dyes, preservatives, harmful ingredients, and they are 90% organic so you can have peace of mind while enjoying these treats.

How we bake

Yeast dough gets an overnight rest when we can spare the time. Cake batters are mixed the day you taste them. Glazes are whisked in small bowls so colors stay bright and flavors stay honest. If something runs out, we’re sorry—and we’ll point you to what just came out of the fryer.

Sugar House

You’ll find us on Highland Drive, wedged between errands and dog walks. Thursday through Sunday mornings are our rhythm: open at nine, close at 2:30pm, or when the case goes quiet. It’s a small window on purpose—we’d rather nail a few hours than stretch a thin day.

Dietary notes

Our kitchen is plant-based. Gluten-friendly treats appear on select days; because we fry and glaze in shared space, we can’t promise a celiac-safe environment. Tell us what you need at the counter—we’ll be straight about what’s possible that morning.

Treats at scale

Catering

Bring a box (or three) to the office, the baby shower, or the team huddle that deserves better than grocery pastries. We bundle by the dozen and can add coffee service when the schedule allows.

Assorted boxes

Our go-to is a mixed dozen: yeast donuts, an old fashioned or two, something filled if the day’s batch allows, and a seasonal glaze for color. Larger orders scale in multiples of twelve so the fryer stays happy and your table looks generous.

  Dozen classic mix — crowd-pleasing variety; we choose the lineup unless you note allergies.
  Two-dozen “meeting upgrade” — wider spread for 15–20 people with napkins and a small sign.
  Seasonal feature add-on — fritter or specialty ring when it’s on the menu; priced daily.

Coffee & batch drinks

Ask about thermal dispensers of drip coffee, cold brew totes, or oat lattes by the tray. We’re a small crew, so beverage catering is limited to windows that don’t overlap our shop rush—Thursdays tend to be kindest before the weekend bustle.

How to order

Online ordering is almost ready—for smaller pickups, call the shop and we’ll slot you in. For weddings, brand activations, or anything over four dozen, call with your date and headcount; we’ll confirm what we can fry and when.

A heads-up of at least 48 hours helps for large orders. Holiday weekends book quickly; the earlier you reach out, the more options you’ll have.
